BSc Computer Science — National and Kapodistrian University of Athens

I am a Computer Science student at NKUA with a focus on Machine Learning and its mathematical foundations. My work sits at the intersection of rigorous theory and real-world application: from understanding the formal underpinnings of learning algorithms to building systems that generate high-quality synthetic data.
I am currently on the academic path towards an MSc.

What I work on

Research Interests

Machine Learning

Foundations and algorithms of supervised, unsupervised, and self-supervised learning, with an emphasis on generalization bounds and theoretical guarantees.

Mathematical ML

Applying tools from probability theory, linear algebra, and optimization to rigorously understand and improve learning systems from first principles.

Synthetic Data Generation

Designing models and evaluation frameworks that produce realistic and synthetic datasets for downstream ML tasks, specifically QA-systems on Knowledge Graphs.

Practical AI Applications

Bridging research and deployment by building robust, real-world AI systems. Turning theoretical insights into tools that solve meaningful problems.
